Funded in the Student Creativity Programme (Ministry of Education, Research and Technology), Gagasan Video Konstruktif category: one of 5,102 funded teams out of 42,368, with IDR 8.5 million, and the only Universitas Padjadjaran team funded in that category. The project tackles the stigma around mental health, focusing on stress, and proposes a wireless sensor glove that detects stress levels.
